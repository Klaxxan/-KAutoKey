About KAutoKey

KAutoKey is a simple, reliable automation utility for Windows that lets you automatically press keyboard keys or perform mouse clicks at customizable intervals. Designed to stay lightweight and clutter-free, it’s perfect for gaming, testing, or automating repetitive tasks without the bloat of heavy automation suites.

Features

  • Two modes: Keyboard or Mouse automation
  • Custom key combos with Ctrl, Alt, and Shift modifiers
  • Adjustable press or click intervals with preset and custom options
  • Choose mouse button type: Left, Right, or Middle
  • Global start/stop toggle key (default: F8)
  • LED-style status indicator showing active or idle state
  • Settings are saved automatically and restored on next launch
  • Clean, modern tabbed interface with themed visuals

How to Use

  1. Open KAutoKey and select your preferred mode: Keyboard or Mouse.
  2. For keyboard mode, choose the key(s) and optional modifiers you want to automate.
  3. For mouse mode, select the click type (Left, Right, or Middle).
  4. Set your interval using one of the presets or a custom time in seconds.
  5. Press Start or use your toggle hotkey (F8 by default) to begin.
  6. Press Stop or the toggle key again to end automation.
  7. Your settings are saved automatically for next time.

It’s that easy lightweight, efficient, and ready to go whenever you need it.
